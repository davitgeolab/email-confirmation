Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Wallpaper mobile application ("Wallpaper", "we", "our", or "the App") handles information when you use the App. Wallpaper lets you browse wallpapers, save favorites, and access optional premium features.

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact davitkokilashvili3@gmail.com.

1. Information we process

Depending on how you use Wallpaper, we and our service providers may process:

  • Account information: your email address, the username you provide, authentication identifiers, and information needed to maintain your account when you register or sign in.
  • Favorites and preferences: wallpapers you save as favorites and associated account identifiers.
  • Download history: when you are signed in and save a wallpaper to your device, we record which wallpaper was saved and when, linked to your account. This is used to show download statistics in your profile.
  • On-device data: your recent search terms are stored only on your device to make searching easier. They are not sent to our servers and are removed when you log out or delete your account.
  • Technical information: information necessary to deliver wallpapers, operate the App, secure services, and diagnose errors, which may include IP address, device or app information, and request logs processed by our providers.
  • Advertising information: when ads are enabled, Google and its partners may process device identifiers, approximate location derived from technical signals, ad interactions, and other information as described in Google's policies, subject to applicable permissions and privacy choices.
  • Subscription information: subscription status and entitlement information used to enable Premium features. Payments are handled by Apple; we do not receive your complete payment card details.

You can browse wallpapers without creating an account. Account information is needed for account-based features such as favorites.

2. How information is used

We use information to provide and maintain Wallpaper, authenticate accounts, sync favorites, show your profile statistics, deliver images, enable purchases and Premium access, prevent abuse, respond to support requests, and display ads to users who do not have an active Premium subscription.

3. Advertising and your choices

Wallpaper uses Google AdMob to display ads to non-Premium users. Where permitted, ads may be personalized based on relevant data and permissions. Otherwise, Google may serve non-personalized or other limited ads, or no ad may be available.

Where required, we ask for advertising privacy choices using Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). This includes consent under European regulations (EEA, UK, and Switzerland) and the choice to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information under applicable US state privacy laws. You can decline consent without purchasing Premium. Where applicable, you can revisit your advertising privacy choices at any time in the App under Profile → Privacy choices. Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission, if requested, is separate from the UMP consent choice.

Learn more about Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information in advertising.

4. Service providers

  • Supabase: authentication, account data, favorites, download history, and the wallpaper catalog. See Supabase's Privacy Policy.
  • Cloudflare: hosting and delivery of wallpaper images and videos, which involves processing technical information such as IP address and request logs. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.
  • Google AdMob: advertising and associated privacy choices. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple: in-app subscriptions, payment processing, and operating-system permissions. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

These providers may process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Data may be processed in countries outside your country of residence, subject to applicable safeguards.

5. Photos and downloads

When you choose to save a wallpaper, the App may request permission to add the image to your photo library. Wallpaper does not need access to your entire photo library solely to save a selected wallpaper. You can manage permissions in your device settings.

Downloading wallpapers is a Premium feature. If you are signed in, each successful download is added to your download history as described in section 1.

6. Data retention and account deletion

We retain account-related information while your account is active and as reasonably necessary to provide services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and protect our services. Retention periods may differ for provider logs and legally required records.

You may request deletion of your account and associated account data, including favorites and download history, through the App's account deletion feature, or contact us at davitkokilashvili3@gmail.com. Some information may be retained where required by law or for legitimate security and compliance purposes. Deleting an account does not automatically cancel an Apple subscription; manage subscriptions in your Apple account settings.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain certain information, object to some processing, withdraw consent, or lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. Contact us to exercise applicable rights. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing already carried out lawfully before withdrawal.

If you live in a US state with a consumer privacy law, you may have the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information for targeted advertising. You can do this in the App under Profile → Privacy choices, where available. We do not sell your account information, such as your email address, favorites, or download history.

9. Children's privacy

Wallpaper is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information improperly, contact us so we can review the request and take appropriate action.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the App, service providers, or legal requirements. The updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised effective date when appropriate.
